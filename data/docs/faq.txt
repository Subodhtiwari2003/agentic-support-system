=== ORDERS ===
 
Q: How do I place an order?
A: To place an order, browse our website and add items to your cart. When you're ready, click the cart icon and proceed to checkout. Enter your shipping address, select a payment method, and confirm your order. You will receive a confirmation email with your order details and a unique order ID within a few minutes.
 
Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?
A: You can modify your order within 1 hour of placing it. Go to My Orders, select the order, and click Edit Order. You can change item quantities, sizes, or colors. After 1 hour, the order is sent to the warehouse and cannot be modified. In that case, you may cancel and reorder.
 
Q: How do I cancel my order?
A: To cancel an order, go to My Account > My Orders and select the order you want to cancel. Click Cancel Order and select a reason. Cancellations are only accepted if the order has not yet been shipped. Once cancelled, a full refund will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.
 
Q: Why was my order cancelled automatically?
A: Orders may be automatically cancelled if payment verification fails, the item goes out of stock after you ordered, or our fraud detection system flags the transaction. You will receive an email explaining the reason. If payment was charged, it will be refunded within 3-5 business days.
 
Q: How do I find my order ID?
A: Your order ID is included in the confirmation email sent to you right after purchase. It is also visible in My Account > My Orders. The order ID starts with ORD followed by 8 digits, for example ORD-20481923.
 
Q: Can I place an order without creating an account?
A: Yes, we support guest checkout. Simply enter your email address and shipping details at checkout without logging in. However, creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, and access order history more easily.
 
Q: What happens if I ordered the wrong item?
A: If you ordered the wrong item and the order has not shipped yet, cancel it immediately from My Orders and reorder the correct item. If the order has already shipped, you can return it once delivered and place a new order.
 
Q: Can I add items to an existing order?
A: Unfortunately, items cannot be added to an existing order after it has been placed. You will need to place a new separate order for the additional items.
 
Q: How many items can I order at once?
A: There is no strict limit for most products. However, some high-demand or limited-edition items may have a maximum purchase quantity of 2-5 units per customer to ensure fair availability. This limit is displayed on the product page if it applies.
 
Q: Do you offer bulk or wholesale orders?
A: Yes, we support bulk orders for businesses. Contact our B2B team at bulk@shopmart.com with your requirements. Orders above 50 units qualify for wholesale pricing with discounts of 15-30% depending on quantity and product category.
 
=== SHIPPING ===
 
Q: What shipping options are available?
A: We offer three shipping options. Standard Delivery takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders above Rs. 499. Express Delivery takes 2-3 business days and costs Rs. 99. Same-Day Delivery is available in select cities for orders placed before 11 AM and costs Rs. 149.
 
Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you will receive an email and SMS with a tracking link and tracking number. You can also track your order by going to My Account > My Orders and clicking Track Order. Real-time updates show the current location of your package.
 
Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days. Express delivery takes 2-3 business days. Same-day delivery is available in select cities. Remote areas may take an additional 2-3 days beyond standard estimates. Delivery times exclude public holidays and weekends.
 
Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to over 30 countries. International shipping typically takes 10-15 business days. International orders are subject to customs duties and taxes which are the buyer's responsibility. Shipping costs for international orders are calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.
 
Q: Why is my tracking not updating?
A: Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update after your order ships. If there is no update for more than 48 hours, the package may be in transit between facilities where scans are less frequent. If tracking has not updated for over 5 days, please contact our support team with your order ID.
 
Q: My order shows delivered but I haven't received it. What should I do?
A: First check around your delivery address including with neighbors or building reception. Sometimes couriers mark packages as delivered slightly early. Wait 24 hours as the package may arrive the next business day. If still not received after 24 hours, contact us at support@shopmart.com with your order ID and we will investigate and reship or refund.
 
Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: You can change your delivery address within 1 hour of placing the order by contacting support immediately. After 1 hour, the order may already be processed for dispatch and address changes are not guaranteed. Once shipped, the address cannot be changed.
 
Q: What happens if I miss the delivery?
A: If you miss the delivery, the courier will attempt delivery up to 2 more times on the following business days. After 3 failed attempts, the package is returned to our warehouse. You can then request a redeliver for a fee of Rs. 49 or receive a full refund minus the shipping cost.
 
Q: Is there free shipping?
A: Yes, standard shipping is free on all orders above Rs. 499 within India. For orders below Rs. 499, standard shipping costs Rs. 49. Express and same-day delivery charges apply regardless of order value.
 
Q: Do you deliver to PO boxes?
A: We do not deliver to PO boxes or military addresses for express or same-day orders. Standard delivery to PO boxes is available in select pin codes. We recommend using a physical street address to ensure smooth delivery.
 
=== RETURNS AND REFUNDS ===
 
Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for most products. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags intact. Electronics must be returned within 10 days. Certain categories like innerwear, perishables, and customized items are not eligible for return.
 
Q: How do I initiate a return?
A: Go to My Account > My Orders and select the order. Click Return Item and select the item and reason for return. Choose between pickup from your address or drop-off at a nearby store. Once your return request is approved, you will receive a return label. Pack the item securely and hand it to the courier.
 
Q: How long does it take to get a refund?
A: Once your returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, the refund is processed within 2-3 business days. It then takes 5-7 additional business days to reflect in your bank account or card. For wallet refunds, it appears within 24 hours after processing.
 
Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
A: Yes, exchanges are available for size or color changes within 30 days of delivery. Go to My Orders > Exchange Item and select the replacement option. The exchange item will be shipped once we receive your original item. Exchanges for different products require a return and fresh purchase.
 
Q: What items cannot be returned?
A: The following items are non-returnable: innerwear and swimwear, perishable food items, digital downloads, gift cards, customized or personalized products, items marked as Final Sale, and hazardous materials. These restrictions are mentioned on the product page at the time of purchase.
 
Q: What if I received a damaged or defective item?
A: If you receive a damaged or defective item, report it within 48 hours of delivery. Go to My Orders > Report Issue and upload photos of the damage. We will arrange an immediate replacement or full refund at no extra cost to you. Do not use or alter the item before reporting the issue.
 
Q: Can I return an item bought during a sale?
A: Yes, sale items are eligible for return unless specifically marked as Final Sale on the product page. The refund will be for the price you actually paid, not the original price. Items marked Final Sale are non-returnable and non-exchangeable.
 
Q: What is your refund method?
A: Refunds are issued to the original payment method by default. If you paid by credit or debit card, the refund goes back to that card. UPI and net banking refunds go to the source account. Cash on delivery refunds are issued as store credit or bank transfer. You can also choose to receive a refund as ShopMart wallet credit for faster processing.
 
Q: I returned my item but haven't received a refund yet. What should I do?
A: First verify that 7-10 business days have passed since we confirmed receipt of your return. Check your bank statements and spam folder for any refund notifications. If you still haven't received it, contact us at support@shopmart.com with your order ID and return tracking number and we will escalate it immediately.
 
Q: Can I return an item if I lost the original packaging?
A: Minor original packaging loss may be acceptable for some product categories. However, electronics, appliances, and fragile items must be returned in original packaging to prevent transit damage. Contact support before initiating a return if you no longer have the original box, and we will advise on a case-by-case basis.
 
=== PAYMENTS ===
 
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), debit cards, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, EMI on select cards, ShopMart wallet, and Cash on Delivery for orders below Rs. 5000.
 
Q: Is Cash on Delivery available?
A: Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to Rs. 5000 in most pin codes. It is not available for international orders, digital products, or express same-day delivery. A COD handling fee of Rs. 25 applies per order.
 
Q: My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
A: If payment was deducted but the order was not confirmed, do not panic. This is typically a bank authorization hold that is automatically reversed within 5-7 business days. If the amount is not reversed after 7 days, share your transaction reference number with us at support@shopmart.com and we will coordinate with the payment gateway.
 
Q: How do I apply a coupon code?
A: At checkout, enter your coupon code in the Promo Code field and click Apply. The discount will be reflected in your order total before you confirm payment. Only one coupon code can be applied per order. Coupon codes are case-sensitive and must be entered exactly as provided.
 
Q: Is it safe to save my card details on the website?
A: Yes, we use PCI-DSS compliant tokenization to store card details. Your actual card number is never stored on our servers. We use industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption for all transactions. You can manage or delete saved cards anytime from My Account > Payment Methods.
 
Q: Can I pay in installments or use EMI?
A: Yes, EMI is available on purchases above Rs. 3000 using select credit cards from HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, and Kotak. EMI options of 3, 6, 9, and 12 months are available. EMI interest rates are shown at checkout. Some products also offer No Cost EMI where the interest is borne by us.
 
=== ACCOUNT AND PROFILE ===
 
Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click Sign Up on the top right of the homepage. Enter your name, email address, and create a password. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox. Your account is ready. You can also sign up using your Google or Facebook account for faster registration.
 
Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click Forgot Password on the login page. Enter your registered email address and click Send Reset Link. Check your inbox for the reset email and click the link within 15 minutes as it expires after that. Enter your new password and confirm it. You will be logged in automatically after reset.
 
Q: How do I update my phone number or email address?
A: Go to My Account > Profile Settings. Click Edit next to the field you want to update. You will be asked to verify the change with an OTP sent to your current registered number or email. Enter the OTP and save the changes.
 
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: To delete your account, go to My Account > Settings > Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm your decision and provide a reason. Note that account deletion is permanent and all your order history, saved addresses, and wallet balance will be lost. Pending refunds will still be processed to your bank account. Accounts with open orders cannot be deleted until all orders are completed or cancelled.
 
Q: I am not receiving OTPs. What should I do?
A: First check that your phone number is correctly registered in your account. Ensure you have network coverage and your SMS inbox is not full. OTPs expire after 5 minutes so request a fresh one if needed. If you still do not receive the OTP, try the Resend OTP option after 30 seconds or contact support to verify your registered number.
 
=== PRODUCTS AND INVENTORY ===
 
Q: How do I know if a product is in stock?
A: Product availability is shown on the product page. If the item is available you will see the Add to Cart button. If out of stock, you will see a Notify Me button. Click it to receive an email alert when the item is back in stock.
 
Q: Can I pre-order out-of-stock items?
A: Pre-orders are available for select products. If pre-order is available, you will see a Pre-Order Now button on the product page along with the expected restock date. Pre-order payments are charged immediately and the item ships as soon as stock is available.
 
Q: Are all products on the website genuine?
A: Yes, all products sold on ShopMart are 100% authentic. We source directly from brands and authorized distributors. We have a zero-tolerance policy for counterfeit goods. If you suspect a product is not genuine, report it to us immediately and we will investigate and replace it.
 
Q: How do I write a product review?
A: You can review a product after your order is delivered. Go to My Orders, find the product, and click Write a Review. Rate it from 1 to 5 stars and share your experience. Reviews are published within 24-48 hours after moderation. Only verified buyers can leave reviews.
